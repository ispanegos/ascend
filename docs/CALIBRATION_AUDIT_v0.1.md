**calibration_status: provisional.** These are internal ASCEND calibration curves. They are not scientific norms, percentiles, population rankings or medical thresholds.

## How scores are formed

1. Each test's raw result becomes a feature (e.g. speed in m/min).
2. Loaded strength features are adjusted for body size where the curve says so (`mixed`: ÷ body mass^0.67, `relative`: ÷ body mass). Raw values stay in the evidence.
3. A curve maps the feature to 0–100 by piecewise-linear interpolation; the end scores are held outside the range.
4. Sources → subdomain → attribute are weighted means over what was measured, then pulled toward the missing-evidence prior for anything measurable that is missing (never zero, never higher).
